Welcome to the DLAB laser cutter! Follow these steps to prepare your design. Not sure about something? The Lab Manager is always happy to help.
01
🖥️

Choose your software

Create your design in vector-based software. These are the best options:

Inkscape (free) Adobe Illustrator AutoCAD Fusion 360 TinkerCAD SolidWorks
580 × 380 mm
Maximum work area

Always keep your design within these dimensions. Draw at 1:1 scale!

02
📐

Prepare your design

Pay attention to these technical details before exporting:

📏 Scale Always 1:1 — what you see is what you get
🛤️ Paths All lines must be paths. Convert text to outlines!
🔗 Contours No intersecting lines, no open contours
🔩 Small details Holes/shapes must be at least 50% of material thickness
💡

Text to path: In Illustrator this is called "Convert to Outlines". In some CAD software it's called "explode" or "expand". Hover over text — if it's selectable as text, it still needs to be converted.

⚠️

Kerf (cut width): The laser removes 0.08–0.1 mm of material per cut. For press-fit parts, you must account for this — otherwise your parts will be too loose!

03
🧱

Choose your material

Start with cardboard if you're making a prototype — cheap and fast. Other options available at DLAB:

📦 Cardboard Ideal for prototypes
🟫 MDF Sturdy, easy to work with
🔷 Acrylic Clean and transparent
🔲 Polycarbonate Strong and flexible
💬

Not sure which material suits your project? Ask the Lab Manager! They know the available thicknesses and materials best.

For assembled designs (e.g. a box with tabs): always account for material thickness in your design.

04
💾

Export your file

Save your design in one of these formats:

.DXF — recommended .SVG .AI .PDF
✅

.DXF works best in our experience. This format accurately preserves all paths and dimensions.

05
🔆

Cutting — together with the Lab Manager

Only trained users may operate the laser cutter independently. Everyone starts together with the Lab Manager:

  • 🗂️ Review the file together
  • ⚙️ Select settings for your material
  • 🧪 Run a material test if needed
  • ✂️ Cut!
06
✨

Post-processing (optional)

  • 🪵 Sand edges / finish engraving
  • 🔧 Glue parts together
  • 🧹 Clean up your workspace — leave it tidy!

3D Printing Manual Use these steps to prepare your model and start printing safely.
1
📦

Prepare the model

Check the model for errors, orient it for best print quality, and generate supports if needed.

2
🧮

Slice with correct settings

Choose the right layer height, infill, print speed, and temperature for your filament.

3
📏

Level the bed

Make sure the print bed is level and the nozzle height is calibrated before starting the print.

4
⏳

Start and monitor

Begin the print and watch the first layer carefully to ensure adhesion and proper extrusion.

💡

Keep the print area clean and avoid touching the hot nozzle or bed while printing.

Soldering Guide Learn the safest and cleanest way to solder electronics.
1
🧰

Prepare your workspace

Work in a well-ventilated area with a heat-resistant mat, fume extractor, and eye protection.

2
🔧

Prep components

Trim leads, clean pads, and tin the tip of the soldering iron before starting.

3
🧪

Solder the joint

Heat the pad and lead, then apply solder until it flows smoothly. Remove the solder first, then the iron.

4
✨

Inspect and clean

Check for shiny, smooth joints and remove flux residue with isopropyl alcohol.

⚠️

Never touch the soldering iron tip and always unplug it after use.
